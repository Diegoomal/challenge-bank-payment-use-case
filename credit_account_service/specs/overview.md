# Credit Account Service

## Business Description

The `credit_account_service` is responsible for crediting the recipient account after the customer's account has been debited successfully.

This service belongs to the Account context and represents the step where the transferred amount becomes available to the merchant/recipient.

It consumes the `debit.completed` event, validates that the recipient account exists, performs the credit, records the accounting entry, and publishes `credit.completed` or `credit.failed`.

## Bounded Context

Account.

## Ubiquitous Language

- Account: the recipient's financial account.
- AccountId: the unique account identifier.
- AccountHolder: the account holder.
- Balance: the available account balance.
- AccountingEntry: accounting entry generated by a movement.
- Credit: balance incoming operation.
- Merchant: the payment recipient.
- DebitCompleted: event that reports that the payer debit was completed successfully.

## Aggregate Root

### Account

The `Account` is the Aggregate Root of the Account context.

It controls the balance and ensures that credit operations are recorded correctly.

## Entities

### AccountingEntry

Represents an accounting entry associated with a credit operation.

## Credit States / Outcomes

- COMPLETED
- FAILED

## Invariants

- A recipient account must exist to receive the credit.
- The credit amount must be greater than zero.
- Every approved credit must generate an accounting entry.
- A credit operation must be associated with a `transaction_id`.
- The same transaction must not be credited more than once.
- The operation must be idempotent by `transaction_id`.

## Main Use Case

### CreditAccount

Responsible for crediting the recipient account balance.

Expected input:

- transaction_id
- customer_id
- merchant_id
- amount
- debited_at

Expected output:

- account_id
- transaction_id
- status
- reason

## Consumed Domain Events

### DebitCompleted

Consumed when the customer's account debit was completed successfully.

Expected payload:

- transaction_id
- account_id
- customer_id
- merchant_id
- amount
- debited_at

## Published Domain Events

### CreditCompleted

Published when the credit is performed successfully.

Suggested payload:

- transaction_id
- account_id
- customer_id
- merchant_id
- amount
- credited_at

### CreditFailed

Published when the credit cannot be performed.

Suggested payload:

- transaction_id
- customer_id
- merchant_id
- amount
- reason
- failed_at

## Ports

### AccountRepository

Responsible for retrieving and saving accounts.

### EventPublisher

Responsible for publishing domain events.

## Responsibilities

This service must:

- Consume the `debit.completed` event.
- Find the account by `merchant_id`.
- Validate that the recipient account exists.
- Credit the amount to the account.
- Record the accounting entry.
- Persist the updated account.
- Publish the `credit.completed` event on success.
- Publish the `credit.failed` event on failure.

This service must not:

- Create payment transactions.
- Debit the payer account.
- Confirm payments.
- Reverse payments.
- Notify customers or merchants.
- Issue receipts.
