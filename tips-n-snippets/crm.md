# CRM

## Summary

CRM means Customer Relationship Management.

It is a system used to manage interactions with customers, leads, prospects, and sales opportunities.

CRM helps companies organize customer data, track communication history, manage sales pipelines, and improve customer relationships.

## When to Use

Use CRM when you need to:

- Manage customer information
- Track leads and sales opportunities
- Organize customer communication
- Improve sales processes
- Follow customer history
- Manage support interactions
- Centralize business relationship data

## Practical Example

```text
1. A lead fills a form on the website
2. The lead is registered in the CRM
3. Sales team contacts the lead
4. Interactions are logged
5. The lead becomes an opportunity
6. The opportunity becomes a customer
```
