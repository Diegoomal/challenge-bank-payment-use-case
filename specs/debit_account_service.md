# Debit Account Service

## Business Description

The `debit_account_service` is responsible for debiting the customer's account after a payment transaction is started.

This service belongs to the Account context and represents the step where the payer's balance is reserved/withdrawn so the payment saga can continue.

It consumes the `payment.started` event, validates that the account has sufficient funds, performs the debit, records the accounting entry, and publishes `debit.completed` or `debit.failed`.

## Bounded Context

Account.

## Ubiquitous Language

- Account: the customer's financial account.
- AccountId: the unique account identifier.
- AccountHolder: the account holder.
- Balance: the available account balance.
- AccountingEntry: accounting entry generated by a movement.
- Debit: balance withdrawal operation.
- InsufficientBalance: domain error raised when the balance is lower than the requested amount.
- PaymentStarted: event that reports that a payment transaction was started.

## Aggregate Root

### Account

The `Account` is the Aggregate Root of the Account context.

It controls the balance and ensures that no operation leaves the account in an invalid state.

## Entities

### AccountingEntry

Represents an accounting entry associated with a debit operation.

## Debit States / Outcomes

- COMPLETED
- FAILED

## Invariants

- An account cannot have a negative balance.
- A debit can only occur if there are sufficient funds.
- The debit amount must be greater than zero.
- Every approved debit must generate an accounting entry.
- A debit operation must be associated with a `transaction_id`.
- The same transaction must not be debited more than once.
- The operation must be idempotent by `transaction_id`.

## Responsibilities

This service must:

- Consume the `payment.started` event.
- Find the account by `customer_id`.
- Validate that the account exists.
- Validate that there are sufficient funds.
- Debit the account amount.
- Record the accounting entry.
- Persist the updated account.
- Publish the `debit.completed` event on success.
- Publish the `debit.failed` event on failure.

This service must not:

- Create payment transactions.
- Credit the recipient account.
- Confirm payments.
- Reverse payments.
- Notify customers or merchants.
- Issue receipts.
